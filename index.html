Hermes Agent

Personal automation tool. Single user, single owner.

What this is

Hermes Agent is a private automation assistant running on one personal computer. It is built and used by its owner only. It schedules reminders, keeps local notes and backups, and talks to services the owner has connected — including his own Google account.

Who uses it

One person: the owner of the account that authorises it. There is no sign-up, no other users, no customers.

What data it touches

  • Files in the owner's own Google Drive — used to keep backup copies of his own documents.
  • The owner's own Gmail, Calendar, Sheets and Docs — read and written only for his own tasks.
  • Data stays on the owner's computer. Nothing is transferred to third parties.
